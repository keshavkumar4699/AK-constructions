Isi samasya ka jawab hai AK Construction. Architect, interior designer aur structure engineer — teeno ek hi chhat ke neeche. Naksha se lekar finishing tak ek hi team zimmedar rehti hai. Isliye humare customers ko middleman ke chakkar nahi lagte.
